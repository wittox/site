FAQ

Questions worth asking

Is the app free?

The app is free to download and use. A one-time Pro unlock removes the limits on batches, file size and output quality. There is no subscription and no advertising.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 and newer. Video work uses the phone's own hardware encoder, so speed depends mostly on the device.

Where do the converted files end up?

In your Downloads folder, next to the original, ready to share from any app.

Who makes it?

Convert 'Em All is built and maintained by The Yellow Apps, an independent Android developer working solo. Bug reports go straight to the person who writes the code.
