Changelog

What changed, and when

Every release that reached Google Play. Older builds stay installable; nothing is taken away from a version you already have.

1.0.4 · October 2026

Opens Italian paperwork: signed .p7m envelopes, FatturaPA electronic invoices and PEC receipts. Read them on screen, or convert them to any document format — a signed envelope is unwrapped first, so an invoice inside one is treated as an invoice. The app also appears in “Open with” when you tap one of those attachments in your mail app.

The interface is now in English, Italian, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese. It follows your phone, asks once on first launch, and can be changed in Settings.

1.0.3 · September 2026

The app is now free: no conversion limit, no in-app purchase, and no billing code left in the build at all. Merge several PDFs into a single document. Batch conversion works through every file you selected in one go.

1.0.1 · July 2026

First release on Google Play, in closed testing. Images, audio, video and documents, converted on the phone, with on-device text recognition.
