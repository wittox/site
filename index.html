Podcast 'Em All

com.yellowapps.podcast_em_all

Give it a PDF, a Word document or the link to an article, and get back either a straight reading in one voice or a podcast where two hosts talk it through, in six languages. Needs a connection, and runs on a monthly plan from €0.99 — readings in the original language stay free.

What they do

Three things worth the space on your phone.

None of this needs a connection. The files never leave the device, so there is nothing to upload, nothing to queue behind somebody else's server, and nothing to trust anybody with.

HEIC → JPG

Formats, converted

The ones a phone actually produces into the ones other people can open. Images, documents, audio and video, in a couple of taps.

PDFs, merged

Pick the files, put them in the order you need, get a single document back. No page limit and no watermark.

Text, recognised

Point it at a photo of a page or a scan and get editable text back — recognised on the phone, in Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Devanagari.

The rules

What I will not do to you.

01

You pay for what costs money

Convert 'Em All works entirely on your phone, so it costs nothing to run and nothing to use. Podcast 'Em All calls a server every time, so it has a plan. Neither one is priced for the sake of it.

02

No adverts, no tracking

There is no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the build. Nothing about how you use the app is collected or sent anywhere.

03

No account

Open it and use it. No sign-up, no email, no login wall standing in front of a file picker.
